Purpose: Check backup freshness and whether scheduled backup protection is operating correctly.

Schedule: Custom 20 * * * * (hourly at minute 20, Singapore time).

Checks local backup receipts, pending delivery to ISS, restore-drill age, required mounts, runtime capabilities and registered schedules. Reports changed problems and recovery, with at most daily reminders for unchanged problems.

Does not start a backup, wake ISS, scan media contents or restart containers.
